App Store Privacy Compliance

🏎️ Blockville: Rush & Ride

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Blockville: Rush & Ride ("the App") is developed as an arcade runner game designed for players of all ages. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency regarding user data.

2. Data Collection & Tracking

We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any email address or personal credentials to play.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: The app does not integrate Google Analytics, Facebook SDKs, or behavioral trackers.
  • No In-App Advertising: We do not include third-party ad networks that monitor device identifiers (IDFA) or browsing history.
  • No Location or Audio Recording: The app does not request access to your GPS location, microphone, or camera.

3. Local Device Storage

The App saves non-personal game progress locally on your device (using standard browser / WebKit local storage APIs). This data includes:

  • High scores and maximum distance traveled
  • In-game star coin and gem counts earned through gameplay
  • Character, vehicle, and palette customization preferences

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app cache.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Apple Family Friendly)

The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple App Store Review Guidelines for Kids & Family categories. Because we collect zero personal information from any user, we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under 13.

5. Haptics and Device Sensors

The App utilizes the device's native Taptic Engine (vibration feedback) and screen touch coordinates solely during active gameplay to provide tactile feedback and responsive arcade controls. These physical signals are processed entirely in real-time on-device and are never recorded.
