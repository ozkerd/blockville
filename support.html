Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my VIP Pass or Remove Ads purchase?
Open the in-game Garage or Pause menu, tap the "👑 VIP PASS" button, and select "RESTORE PURCHASES". If you previously purchased on the same Apple ID, your purchase will be restored immediately at zero cost.
2. Is an internet connection required to play?
No. Blockville: Rush & Ride is fully playable offline. An internet connection is only needed if you wish to watch optional rewarded video ads or purchase in-app items.
3. How do the controls work on iPhone and iPad?
  • Steer: Swipe Left or Right to change lanes.
  • Jump: Swipe Up to jump over hurdles.
  • Slide / Duck: Swipe Down to duck under overhead arches and laser gates.
  • Nitro Boost: Collect rocket canisters, then tap the Boost button or hold the screen.
