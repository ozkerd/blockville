App Store Privacy Compliance

🏎️ Blockville: Rush & Ride

Effective Date: November 2026 | Legal Entity: BTPSEC LTD

1. Introduction

Blockville: Rush & Ride ("the App"), provided by BTPSEC LTD, is designed for players of all ages. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent commitment to user data privacy.

2. Data Collection & Tracking

We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any email address or personal credentials to play.
  • No Identity Linking: Device advertising identifiers (IDFA) are never linked to personal identity or user accounts.
  • Third-Party Advertising: The app utilizes Apple-authorized ad networks (Google AdMob) solely to serve in-game reward ads (e.g., watch ad for revive). Ad tracking requires explicit user opt-in via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • No Location or Audio Recording: The app never requests access to your GPS location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

3. Local Device Storage

The App saves non-personal game progress locally on your device (using standard on-device storage APIs). This data includes:

  • High scores and maximum distance traveled
  • In-game star coin and gem balances earned through gameplay
  • Character, vehicle, and palette customization preferences

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by deleting the app.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Apple Family Guidelines)

The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple App Store Review Guidelines. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13.

5. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

In-app purchases (such as "VIP Pass / Remove Ads") are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit 2. BTPSEC LTD does not collect, receive, or store any financial or credit card information.

6. Customer Support

Customer Support & Inquiries:
For technical inquiries, bug reports, or purchase support, please submit a request via our App Store product page or your Apple purchase receipt.
